Premium Visual Upgrade Audit

Goal:
Review the project’s visuals and explain how to make it look more high-end, polished, and professional.

This audit should focus only on visual quality, presentation, and polish.

Please check:

1. Overall visual impression

* Does the project look cheap, unfinished, or placeholder?
* What makes it feel low-end?
* What parts already look good?
* What should be improved first?

2. Art style

* Is there a clear visual style?
* Do all assets look like they belong together?
* Are any assets mismatched, blurry, stretched, too simple, or low quality?
* What style direction would make the project look better?

3. Color and lighting

* Are the colors attractive and consistent?
* Is the contrast strong enough?
* Does the project need better lighting, shadows, gradients, glow, or atmosphere?
* Are important elements visually separated from the background?

4. UI quality

* Do buttons, menus, icons, text, panels, and counters look professional?
* Is the spacing clean?
* Are fonts readable and consistent?
* Does the UI feel modern or basic?
* What UI elements should be redesigned?

5. Animation and visual feedback

* Are actions visually satisfying?
* Are there enough effects for clicks, hits, rewards, upgrades, damage, success, and failure?
* Are animations too stiff, missing, or too simple?
* What small effects would make the project feel more premium?

6. Backgrounds and environment

* Does the world feel empty or flat?
* Does the background support the main content without distracting?
* Are there enough details, depth, shadows, or atmosphere?
* What background improvements would give the biggest visual upgrade?

7. Prioritized improvement list
   Please provide:

* Top 5 visual problems
* Top 5 quick wins
* Top 5 high-impact upgrades
* What should be changed first
* What can wait until later

Output:
Write the audit results into an audit folder.

Use these files:

* audit/visual_summary.md
* audit/visual_problems.md
* audit/quick_wins.md
* audit/high_impact_upgrades.md
* audit/visual_priority_plan.md

Keep the feedback practical. Focus on changes that would make the project look more polished and high-end without redesigning the whole project from scratch.
